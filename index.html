Needs-based retail sales — case study

Reflection and proposed framework based on Todd Harris Co. Inc experience

Context: As a sales associate, I helped customers select pool and spa products based on budget and needs, explained maintenance and filtration, and performed water tests.

Business question: How can a sales associate make technical product choices clearer for customers?

  1. Understand the need: Ask about the customer’s equipment, maintenance concern, and budget before recommending a product.
  2. Explain the options: Compare relevant choices in plain language and connect each recommendation to the customer’s stated needs.
  3. Confirm understanding: Review the next steps and invite questions so the customer leaves with a clear plan.

Takeaway: Product knowledge becomes more useful when paired with listening and clear communication. A useful future measure would be repeat questions or returns caused by misunderstanding a recommendation.

Experience reference: Todd Harris Co. Inc, Sales Associate, June 2024–August 2025; see the experience section below. This case study proposes a framework and does not claim measured sales improvements.

Restaurant team coordination — case study

Reflection and proposed framework based on Tony’s Pizza & Restaurant experience

Context: My busboy role included clearing tables, washing dishes, delivering food, refilling drinks, responding to requests, and taking orders. I supported two waitresses during short-staffed service and collaborated with a team of ten other workers.

Business question: How can team members coordinate competing tasks while maintaining a welcoming dining environment?

  1. Identify immediate needs: Notice waiting customers, ready food, and tables that need clearing.
  2. Communicate priorities: Coordinate with waitstaff before switching tasks to reduce missed requests and duplicate effort.
  3. Support across roles: Use an understanding of coworkers’ responsibilities to help where demand is greatest.

Takeaway: Reliable operations depend on shared awareness and flexible support. A useful future measure would be the time between a customer leaving and the table being ready for the next guest.

Experience reference: Tony’s Pizza & Restaurant, Busboy, January 2021–December 2024; see the experience section below. This case study proposes an approach and does not claim a formally implemented improvement project.
